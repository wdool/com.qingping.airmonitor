Stuffy room, tired head? Qingping Air Monitor brings the readings of your Qingping air quality monitors into Homey, so you always know how fresh the air in your home really is. CO₂, fine dust (PM2.5 and PM10), temperature and humidity show up on your devices and in Insights, so you can spot patterns over days and weeks.

Let Homey act on it: get a notification, switch on the ventilation or start the air purifier as soon as CO₂ or fine dust rises above a level you choose.
