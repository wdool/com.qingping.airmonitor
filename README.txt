Monitor your indoor air quality with Qingping sensors directly in Homey.

Supported sensors:
- Qingping Air Monitor Lite (CGS1)
- Qingping Air Monitor (CGDN1)
- Any Qingping/Cleargrass device with CO₂, PM2.5, PM10, temperature and humidity

Features:
- Live readings for CO₂, PM2.5, PM10, temperature and humidity
- Insights graphs for all measurements
- Flow triggers when CO₂, PM2.5 or PM10 exceed a threshold
- Flow conditions to check current levels
- Configurable poll interval (1–60 minutes)

Setup:
1. Create an OAuth app at developer.qingping.co
2. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret
3. Add a device in Homey and enter those credentials
4. All your Qingping devices will be discovered automatically
